Tuffet.

Colouring and stories, for small children

Tuffet is a quiet app for children who are about three to seven. Pictures to colour in, and stories read aloud — old folk tales, fables and rhymes, narrated and sorted onto shelves.

It is built to be worked by a child on their own. Big targets, no menus to get lost in, nothing that punishes a wrong tap. Stories download once and then play without a signal, so a car journey or a bedroom with bad reception is no obstacle.

What it does not do

  • No advertising, of any kind
  • No accounts and no sign-in
  • No analytics and no tracking
  • No personal information collected, from children or from adults

There is one optional purchase, which unlocks the whole library for good. It sits behind a question a young child cannot answer, so nobody buys it by accident. Everything else is free, and nothing expires.
