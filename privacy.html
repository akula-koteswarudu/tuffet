Tuffet.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Tuffet collects no personal information from anyone, child or adult. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Everything a child makes in the app stays on their device.

Tuffet is a colouring and audio-story app made for young children. Because it is for children, it is held to a higher standard than most apps, and the simplest way to meet that standard is not to gather anything in the first place. This policy describes what the app does, what leaves the device, and what does not.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses
  • Photographs, camera or microphone input
  • Location, of any kind or precision
  • Contacts, calendar or any other content on the device
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers or persistent profiles
  • Analytics, usage statistics or behavioural data

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no crash-reporting service and no social-media integration. There is no sign-up, so there is nothing for a child to fill in and nothing for a parent to consent to.

What stays on the device

Some things are saved so that the app remembers where a child left off. All of it is stored in the app's own private storage and none of it is ever sent anywhere:

  • Coloured-in pictures and drawings
  • Which stories have been downloaded and last listened to
  • Settings such as sound on or off, playback speed, and downloading over Wi-Fi only

Uninstalling Tuffet deletes all of it permanently. There is no copy on a server, because none was ever made.

Downloading stories

Story audio is not bundled with the app; it is downloaded when a story is opened. That download is an ordinary web request to our content server, which is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

Like every web server, it records the technical details of the requests it receives, which include the IP address the request came from, the time, and which file was asked for. We do not use these records to identify or track anyone, we do not combine them with anything else, and the app sends no account, name or identifier alongside the request. Cloudflare processes these logs as our hosting provider under their privacy policy.

The app makes no other network connections of any kind.

Purchases

Tuffet offers one optional purchase that unlocks the full story library. It is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see and never receive card numbers, billing addresses or any payment details.

Google Play tells the app only whether the signed-in Google account owns the unlock. The app remembers that single yes-or-no answer on the device so the stories still work when there is no signal. Payments are governed by Google's privacy policy.

The purchase is placed behind a question that a young child cannot answer, so that a purchase is not made by accident.

Permissions

Tuffet asks the device for two things:

  • Internet — to download story audio.
  • Vibrate — for the small buzz when a picture is finished.

It requests no access to storage, photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

Tuffet is designed for children and participates in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR, including its provisions for children, by not collecting personal information from anyone.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held about a child that could be disclosed, sold or requested. Should you still wish to raise a question about your child's use of the app, write to us at the address below.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share data with anyone, because we hold none. The only third parties involved are Cloudflare, which serves the story files, and Google Play, which handles the optional purchase. Neither receives anything from us about you.

Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA you may ask what personal data is held about you, request a copy, or ask for it to be deleted. In Tuffet's case the answer to all three is the same: we hold none. Everything the app saves is on your own device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. If a future version were ever to collect personal information, we would say so here plainly and ask for consent in the app before doing it.
